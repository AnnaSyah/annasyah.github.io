Anna Syahrani is a lecturer in the Department of Informatics Engineering at Institut Teknologi Padang, Indonesia. Her research interests include Context-Aware Computing and ICT for Society, where she explores and develops intelligent systems that leverage context-aware computing and machine learning to address societal challenges.

She participated in a lecturer internship program at the Department of Electrical Engineering and Information Technology, Universitas Gadjah Mada (UGM), in 2022, supported by the Kampus Merdeka Competition Program (PKKM), Ministry of Education, Culture, Research, and Technology of the Republic of Indonesia.

She was also involved in the Smart Building for Energy Saving in Indonesia (SuvI) project, a research collaboration between Universitas Gadjah Mada, Indonesia, and the University of Groningen, the Netherlands from 2013-2015. The project aimed to improve building energy efficiency through pervasive sensor networks and context-aware systems.
