4. **AdaptiveUX: Machine Learning-Based User Interface Customization Engine.** Registration Number: EC002025XXXXXX, 18 September 2025.
5. **EdgeContext-App: Lightweight Ambient Intelligence Application for Android Smartphones.** Registration Number: EC002026XXXXXX, 05 May 2026.
